Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

MailSage is a local email-analysis application. This policy describes how the application accesses, processes, and stores information when you connect a Gmail account and choose messages to analyze.

In brief: MailSage uses Google OAuth to access your Gmail, stores fetched message data and its authorization token on the device running the application, and uses a local AI model to analyze messages you select. MailSage does not send email content to a hosted AI service.

Information MailSage accesses

When you authorize Gmail access and fetch a date range, MailSage retrieves messages in that range. Depending on the message, this can include sender and recipient details, subject, date, labels, snippet, and the text and HTML body. The application requests Gmail's gmail.modify permission so it can remove the UNREAD label when you open a message or successfully generate its summary.

You choose which fetched messages to analyze. Their available message content is passed to the local MailSage backend and processed by the local Qwen model.

How information is used

  • To display Gmail messages that fall within the date range you select.
  • To generate summaries and identify useful details in the messages you choose, using a model running locally.
  • To update a message's read status in Gmail and in the local cache when you open it or its summary completes successfully.

Email content is not submitted to an external AI provider by MailSage. Google processes OAuth authorization and Gmail API requests under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Where information is stored

The Gmail OAuth token is stored in the application's local .secrets/gmail-token.json file. Fetched messages are cached in the local SQLite database at .data/gmail-cache.sqlite3 and exported to emails.jsonl in the project directory. These files remain on the device where MailSage is running, subject to that device's security, backups, and access controls.

MailSage does not provide cloud storage for this information. You can remove local copies by deleting the relevant files after closing the application. Revoking MailSage's Google access from your Google Account removes future authorization; deleting a local token alone does not delete copies of messages in the cache or export.

Sharing and disclosure

MailSage does not sell personal information or share message content with advertising or hosted AI services. Message data is sent to Google when the application fetches messages or updates their read status. Local files may also be accessible to people or software that can access your device.

Security and permissions

MailSage runs its local API on the same device as the application. Protect that device and its local files with appropriate account and operating-system security. The Gmail permission is used for fetching messages and marking selected messages as read; MailSage does not use it to send email.

If you previously authorized MailSage with read-only access, Google may ask you to grant consent again for the updated permission.

Children's privacy

MailSage is a general-purpose email utility and is not designed to collect information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when MailSage's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.

Contact

For questions about this policy or MailSage's handling of data, contact the project maintainer through the channel where you obtained the application.
